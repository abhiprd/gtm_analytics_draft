## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Executive-summary narrative for the weekly readout (`analytics/executive_summary.py`, pipeline node `executive_summary`). The readout is passed whole, as delimited data, to the Claude API (`anthropic` 0.86.0, a tool call for structured output, prompt caching on the data block; default model `claude-sonnet-5-5`, overridable with `ACME_SUMMARY_MODEL`), which returns 1-6 statements each citing the readout objects it uses. A deterministic, LLM-free validator (19 checks) accepts or rejects the set before anything is published: every figure must appear, within its displayed rounding and with its unit (currency, percent, multiple, count) and sign handled, in an object the statement cites; dates, quarters, months, years, account ids and cites must exist in the readout; the variance engine's top-ranked driver must be named with its Layer-1 parent and its single-candidate / no-Layer-3 status; caveated and Not-computable nodes must be labelled as such; Layer labels must match tree depth; segment, forecast-lens and metric labels must sit with their own figures where that is checkable; a late-quarter CRO-adjusted read must carry its caveat; "tier", legacy segment names, non-USD currency, forward-looking, speculative and causal wording are rejected. The checks do not verify that the prose is the right story or a causal explanation. A failure is fed back once; a second failure publishes nothing (`validation_failed`). Without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` the section is `not_generated` (reason `no_api_key`); there is no template fallback. A generated summary is cached against the sha256 of the readout it was generated from and reused while that is unchanged.
- `executive_summary` slot contract in the readout JSON: `status` (`generated` / `not_generated` / `validation_failed`), `statements`, `model`, `generated_at`, `input_hash`, `prompt_version`, `validation`, `reason`. The Markdown renders validated statements with a provenance line, or the status and reason with no prose.
- Pipeline: a node may print `[SKIP]` to be recorded as skipped (never passed), and may declare the skip expected so `--strict` lists it without failing; `executive_summary` uses both. Freshness contract entry `executive_summary` (weekly, 10 days) whose evidence is a performance-log checkpoint written on every run, so a deployment without a key stays fresh while `narrative_generated = 0` and the readout's own status show that nothing was generated.

- Weekly readout `segment_mix` section ("are we moving upmarket"): each segment's share of ending MRR with the change against the prior month and 12 months earlier, the Commercial-plus-Enterprise share, migration events and velocity in the month, over the trailing 12 months and over the 12 months a year earlier, and graduated MRR with its share of the source segment's MRR. Four caveats state that migration only moves accounts up, so rates and shares are the comparable measures. Six caveats, two of them carrying the month's own figures (a 12-month graduated flow over an average base can exceed 100% because migrating accounts carry about 6 times the MRR of the average SMB account; the upmarket share is partly reclassification of existing MRR, so it answers where MRR is booked more than who the company sells to). Checks `segment_mix_section_is_built_and_declares_its_status`, `segment_mix_traces_exactly_to_a_fresh_run`, `segment_mix_shares_tie_to_ending_mrr` and `segment_mix_section_rendered_per_status`; `tests/test_phase4_segment_mix.py` (independent recompute from the marts for all 65 reportable months, point-in-time poisoning, the unavailable paths) and `tests/test_weekly_readout_sections.py`.
- Executive-summary validator check `persistence_claims_match_cited_drilldown` (19 checks): a sentence claiming a streak or persistence must name the Layer-2 driver of a cited drill-down (by label, or `NRR's expansion share`-style forms) and agree with that record for every driver it names: a stated length in any of many phrasings (`for the last 4 months`, `second month running`) equal to `streak_months`, `since <month>` equal to `first_month_of_streak`, a bare repeat word (persist, again, recurring, sustained, repeat) only for a flagged record, no improvement or opposite-direction wording, no one-off wording for a flagged record, no favorable streaks. It is a vocabulary and pattern check that a paraphrase can evade; the residual limits are in the methods doc.

- The weekly readout's `executive_summary` slot no longer carries a deferred placeholder: `assemble_readout()` emits `not_generated` / `pending_generation`, re-assembly carries forward a still-valid generated summary, and the two readout checks `narrative_is_deferred_not_generated` and `narrative_placeholder_rendered` became `executive_summary_slot_honors_contract` and `executive_summary_rendered_per_status` (26 checks with `not_computable_count_matches_scorecard_status`).
